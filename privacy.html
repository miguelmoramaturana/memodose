Privacy Policy

Your medications stay with you.

Effective date: October 1, 2026

MemoDose keeps your medications and history only on your iPhone and, if you use it, your Apple Watch. Prescription photos are sent to an artificial intelligence service only to read them, and we do not store them. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and what you can do about it.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your data is Miguel Mora Maturana, based in Chile. You can contact us at hello@memodose.app about any privacy matter.

2. What data we use

Account data. When you sign in with Apple, we receive a user identifier and your email address. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive a relay address instead of your real email.

Prescription photos and documents. When you scan a prescription, a package or a PDF, the image is sent to our server and from there to Anthropic to extract the medications, doses and schedules. We ask for your permission before the first scan. We do not store the image or the result on our server: the result goes back to your phone for you to review.

Technical scan log. For each scan we store the date, the outcome (success or error), the number of images, the number of medications detected, the AI model used, the processing time and the cost. It does not include the photo or the names of the medications. We use it to apply the daily scan limit, detect errors and control costs.

Medications, schedules and dose history. These are stored only on your iPhone. We do not send them to our servers. If you use MemoDose on Apple Watch, your iPhone sends today's doses directly to the watch, and what you mark on the watch goes back to the iPhone, without going through our servers. They may be included in your device backups (iCloud or your computer), depending on your settings.

Subscription. Apple processes the payment. We never receive your card details. The app only asks Apple whether your subscription is active.

Notifications. Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your iPhone and your Apple Watch. They do not go through our servers.

We do not use analytics or advertising tools, we do not track you across apps, and we do not sell your data.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Creating and maintaining your account: to perform our contract with you.
  • Reading your prescriptions with AI: based on your explicit consent, because a prescription contains health data. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings by turning off "AI scanning". You can still add medications manually.
  • Technical scan log: our legitimate interest in keeping the service working, preventing abuse and keeping its costs sustainable.
  • Managing your subscription: to perform our contract with you.

We may also disclose data when required by law or by a competent authority.

5. International transfers

Anthropic and Supabase process data in the United States. When data leaves the European Economic Area or your country, we rely on the safeguards these providers offer, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and scan log: for as long as you keep your account. If you delete it, they are erased immediately.
  • Photos: we do not store them. Anthropic may keep them for a limited time under its terms.
  • Data on your iPhone and Apple Watch: until you delete it, delete your account or uninstall the app.

7. How to delete your account

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This erases your account, your scan log and the data stored on your phone. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke MemoDose's access to your Apple ID. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription: cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, to receive it in a portable format, to restrict or object to its use, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Write to hello@memodose.app; we reply within 30 days at most. You can also file a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). On the server, each person can only access their own records. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

10. Children

MemoDose is designed for adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe someone under 18 has given us their data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Medical disclaimer

MemoDose helps you remember your doses. It is not a medical device and does not replace your doctor or pharmacist. Always review what the AI reads before confirming it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version with its date. If the change is significant, we will let you know in the app.
