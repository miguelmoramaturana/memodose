Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?+

In the app, go to Settings → Manage subscription. You can also do it from your iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → MemoDose. If you cancel, the reminders you already have keep ringing.

I already paid, but the app asks me to subscribe+

Tap "Restore purchases" on the subscription screen or in Settings. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I delete my account?+

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. Your account and data are permanently erased. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription: cancel it separately with Apple so you aren't charged.

My reminders don't show up+

In your iPhone Settings → Notifications → MemoDose, check that notifications are allowed. Also check that no Focus mode is silencing the app.

How do I turn off AI scanning?+

In the app, go to Settings → AI scanning. With scanning off, you can still add your medications manually.

How do I use MemoDose on Apple Watch?+

You need MemoDose on your iPhone and an Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later. Install it from the Watch app on your iPhone, under available apps. The watch shows today's doses and lets you mark them as taken, skip them or snooze them.

What happens to my data if I switch iPhones?+

Your medications and history are stored on your iPhone, not on our servers. They move to your new phone if you restore a backup or use iPhone-to-iPhone transfer.
